About Me

Finding my place in UX

I'm in my final year at Siena University, pursuing a double degree in Computer Science with a cybersecurity track and Interdisciplinary Studies in Human-Centered Experience Design.

I've had the opportunity to explore design in a lot of different ways, from graphic design and photography to UX/UI projects. I enjoy thinking about how people interact with technology and figuring out how design can make those experiences better.

Through these experiences, I've found that UX is where I want to take my career. I'm excited to keep growing as a UX designer and see where this path takes me after graduation.
